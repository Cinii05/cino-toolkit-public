Privacy policy

Effective date: 17 September 2026

Publisher: Max Cini

What Cino Toolkit is

Cino Toolkit is a skills-only plugin. It provides reusable instructions for analysis, product-design review, video analysis, guided planning, programming tuition, and writing assistance.

Data collected by the publisher

The public Cino Toolkit bundle does not operate a publisher-controlled backend, user account system, analytics service, advertising system, contact form, or payment system. Max Cini does not independently receive or store the content that users submit to ChatGPT or Codex merely because they use Cino Toolkit.

OpenAI and connected services

Text, files, images, video, links, and other material that a user provides in ChatGPT or Codex are handled by OpenAI under the terms, privacy settings, and product controls that apply to that user's OpenAI account. If the user separately chooses to use a connected third-party app or service, that provider's own terms and privacy policy may also apply.

Permissions and external actions

Cino Toolkit does not grant itself new permissions and does not bypass existing access controls. The public skills are designed to disclose evidence gaps and not to perform consequential external writes without user authorisation and an available permitted capability.

Sensitive information

Users should avoid providing secrets or personal information that is not necessary for the task. Where a task involves private, confidential, copyrighted, personal, or regulated material, users remain responsible for ensuring they have the right and appropriate basis to provide and use that material.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the plugin's data handling or technical architecture changes. The effective date on this page will be updated when a material change is published.

Contact

Support and privacy enquiries can be raised through the Cino Toolkit GitHub issue tracker.
