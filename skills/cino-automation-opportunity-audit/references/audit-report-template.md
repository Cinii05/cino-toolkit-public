## Value scenarios

| Input or result | Low | Base | High | Evidence or assumption |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|

Show formulas and separate time capacity, avoidable cash cost, recoverable gross contribution, one-time cost, recurring cost, net cash benefit, and cash payback. Do not add capacity value to cash benefit without measured, non-duplicated conversion into a real cash result.

Use **not calculable yet** where an essential input cannot be bounded. Do not fill every scenario cell with invented assumptions.
